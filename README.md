# Conversational AI narrows political information seeking without detectable changes in knowledge or attitudes

Slides for a five-minute talk on a pre-registered field experiment run during the 2026 US midterm elections.

View the slides: https://cjbarrie.github.io/midterms_ai_slides/

Michelle Schimmel, Michael Heseltine, Christopher Bail, Petter Törnberg, Christopher Barrie.

Results are preliminary. The slide on types of election information is exploratory and awaits a human check of the classifier. Screenshots on slide 2 are of public pages by Anthropic, Campaigns & Elections and Google, and belong to their owners.
